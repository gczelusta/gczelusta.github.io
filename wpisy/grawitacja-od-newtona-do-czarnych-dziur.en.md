# Gravity, from Newton to black holes

A three-part tour of how our picture of gravity grew up — from Newton's apple to the event horizon. No heavy maths, but a focus on *how the way of thinking changed*.

## Outline

1. **Part 1 — Newton: a force at a distance.** The law of universal gravitation and its spectacular successes (and what troubled Newton himself).
2. **Part 2 — Einstein: curved spacetime.** Gravity as geometry, not force. Why light bends and time slows.
3. **Part 3 — Black holes.** What happens when curvature becomes extreme, and why they are today's laboratory for quantum gravity.

## Why it's worth it

Because gravity is the one of the four forces we still **cannot reconcile** with quantum mechanics. Understanding how we arrived at today's picture is the best introduction to the questions I work on every day.
