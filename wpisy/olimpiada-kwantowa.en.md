# Quantum Olympiad

![Surface](assets/images/blog/ok_logo.png)

We invite all secondary school students in Poland to take part in the first edition of the Quantum Olympiad and in the workshops preparing for it!

The Quantum Olympiad is a nationwide competition for students interested in mathematics, physics, computer science, and quantum technologies.

If you want not only to learn about the subject, but also to test your knowledge and take on Olympiad-level problems, join our free workshops preparing you for the Quantum Olympiad.

💻 **16 Saturday online sessions** will be led by scientists and professionals working in the field of quantum technologies.

During the workshops, you will learn about:

* complex numbers and linear algebra,
* the basics of quantum mechanics,
* qubits, gates, and quantum circuits,
* quantum algorithms,
* entanglement and Bell’s theorem,
* quantum cryptography and metrology,
* how quantum computers are built,
* quantum error correction and software.

📅 **Workshops start:** October 10, 2026, at 11:00 AM

💻 **Online** | 💰 **Free of charge**

You don’t have to decide right away whether to take part in the Quantum Olympiad — you can join the workshops, explore the subject, and see if it’s something for you. Teachers are also welcome to participate in the workshops.

And if you decide to take part: **registration for the Quantum Olympiad is open until February 28, 2027.**
