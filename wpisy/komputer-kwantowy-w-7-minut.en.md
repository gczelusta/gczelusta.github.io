# A quantum computer in 7 minutes

This is a video post. Below is a short intro and a chapter list — drop in the film itself by changing the address in `dane/wpisy.js` (the `wideoUrl` field), or by pasting a YouTube embed code here.

> **How to embed the video:** in this post's Markdown file, paste the `<iframe>` code from YouTube (*Share → Embed*) and it will appear in the body.

## What the video covers

No equations — with a coin and a bit of patience, I explain:

1. **Bit vs qubit** — how a classical one differs from a qubit.
2. **Superposition** — how a qubit can be "in between".
3. **Measurement** — why looking spoils the fun (and when it works in our favour).
4. **Why bother** — which problems a quantum computer really speeds up.

## Who it's for

Anyone who has heard the phrase "quantum computer" and finally wants to understand the fuss — without university maths.
